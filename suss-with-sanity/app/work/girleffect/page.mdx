## Overview

The campaign for **Wazzii**, launched on Tuesday, November 21st, 2023, marked its debut on the airwaves of **NRG Radio** and **Ghetto Radio**. With a strategic blend of presenter mentions, spot ads, and promos, the campaign permeated the auditory landscape, capturing the attention of diverse audiences. Leveraging the power of social media, both radio stations amplified the message by sharing Wazzii content across their respective online platforms.

Notably, Ghetto Radio offered an additional layer of engagement by hosting an
interview on Monday, December 18th, during the Stella (Mid-Morning) Show. This
interview provided a valuable opportunity for the Girl Effect team to delve

understanding and connection with the initiative.

synergy of radio airplay and digital outreach, laying a strong foundation for the
journey ahead.
