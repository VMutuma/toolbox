## Overview

<br />

**Indigo Homes**, a leading real estate developer in Kenya, collaborated with **Suss Ads** to create a successful digital marketing campaign. The main objective was to generate leads for their upcoming housing development. This case study examines the strategies, tactics, and outcomes of the six-month campaign.

<br />

**Client: Indigo Homes**

<br />

[Website: Indigo Homes](https://indigohomes.com/?utm_source=suss_ads&utm_medium=casestudies&utm_campaign=indigo_homes&utm_id=suss_ads)

<br />

## Campaign Objective

<br />

- To acquire leads for Indigo Homes' development, aiming for **500 high-quality leads**.
- To enhance brand visibility among the Diaspora community and potential homebuyers in Kenya.
  <br />

## Channels Used

<br />

- **Google Search** targeting Diaspora and Kenya
- **Meta** (formerly Facebook) for Diaspora and Kenya
- **Programmatic advertising** (targeting Diaspora and Kenya)
- **LinkedIn**
  <br />

## Target Audience

<br />

To ensure the campaign resonated with the right audience, we targeted the **Diaspora community** and **potential homebuyers in Kenya**. We crafted customized messaging for each segment to maximize relevance and engagement.

**Google Ads**

<br />

Using Google Ads, we targeted users actively searching for real estate and housing solutions. We used relevant keywords like "Kenya real estate," "housing in Kenya," and "property investment Kenya" to attract potential leads with high intent. Additionally, we continuously conducted **A/B testing** on ad copies and landing pages to optimize for improved conversion rates.

<br />

**Meta (Facebook)**

<br />

We targeted users on Meta who are interested in Kenya and the Diaspora. We used **display ads** and **promoted posts** to raise awareness about Indigo Homes. Our strategy included engaging users with compelling visuals and informative content.

<br />

**Programmatic Advertising**

<br />

We strategically placed programmatic Suss ads across relevant websites and apps to ensure visibility of where potential homebuyers are likely to be. **Retargeting** was a key tactic, allowing us to re-engage users who had previously shown interest in real estate or property-related content. The ads appeared on top real estate and news websites, significantly increasing exposure.

<br />

**LinkedIn**

<br />
On LinkedIn, we focused on reaching professionals, investors, and decision-makers.
Sharing success stories, testimonials, and project updates helped build credibility
and trust in Indigo Homes. Notable interactions included engagement from industry
leaders and potential investors, boosting the campaign's credibility.

## **Campaign Results**

<br />
The campaign succeeded, surpassing **90% of the lead acquisition KPI** and generating
**450 high-quality leads**. Indigo Homes received many inquiries and leads from interested
buyers, substantially expanding their potential customer base. The brand also experienced
a noticeable increase in visibility among the Diaspora community and the Kenyan audience,
with a **30% rise in website traffic** and a **25% growth in social media followers**.

<br />

## Conclusion

The Indigo Homes digital campaign combined **creativity, data-driven insights, and strategic planning** to achieve remarkable results. By addressing challenges head-on and maintaining a consistent presence, we helped Indigo Homes connect with their target audience and drive lead acquisition. Future campaigns will focus on maintaining this momentum and further optimizing strategies for even greater success.
