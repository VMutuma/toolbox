Africa's iGaming sector is one of the most enthralling world markets and is growing rapidly thanks to the continent's youthful population, extensive adoption of mobile technologies, evolving regulatory environment, and cultural propensity for gaming and betting. As the market develops and expands, astute investors and business owners can benefit greatly from tapping into this burgeoning sector.

With the mutual objective of supporting iGaming, sportsbook, and online casino companies seeking to expand into the African market, [1Stop Translations](https://www.1stoptranslations.com/) and Suss Ads have joined forces through a collaborative partnership; offering new entrants a seamless entry into the African iGaming landscape.

This partnership will integrate the expertise of [1Stop Translations](https://www.1stoptranslations.com/) in the field of translations and international digital marketing with that of Suss Ads, a Nairobi, Kenya-based marketing and communication agency with cutting-edge marketing technologies such as their Programmatic Ads and SMS advertising platforms. Thanks to this partnership and the integration of services offered, customers will be able to plan marketing campaigns for the entire African continent, localized in the target languages with defined and measurable objectives. [1Stop Translations](https://www.1stoptranslations.com/) is a premier provider of translation and localization services with over 10 years of experience in the industry. They specialize in providing top-notch translation services in 100 languages to a range of industries, including iGaming, sportsbook, fintech, and legal.

Suss Ads, on the other hand, has been awarded on multiple instances and categories for its expertise in strategy and execution of successful campaigns through cutting-edge digital media solutions. They are renowned for helping businesses grow by providing tailored and targeted advertising campaigns that drive results. With a demonstrated track record of delivering successful campaigns for their clients, they consistently prove their ability to drive impactful outcomes.

**Nicola Pansini**, 1Stop Translations Sales and Marketing Manager says: "We are proud to announce this agreement because both companies are dedicated to providing their customers with the best possible service. Through this partnership, we and Suss Ads aim to offer a seamless and efficient solution that will enable our customers to expand the business into the African market with ease".

**Dennis Maina**, Suss Ads Managing Partner, says: "We are excited to join forces with 1Stop Translations. Through this partnership, our specialization in Programmatic Ads and SMS advertising campaigns will be seamlessly integrated with 1Stop Translations' expertise in translations and international digital marketing. Together, we will offer a comprehensive solution that leverages technology and linguistic proficiency to empower our clients to reach and engage with their target audience effectively in the African market and beyond."
